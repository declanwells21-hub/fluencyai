About Fluency AI

Last updated: September 21, 2026

What Fluency AI is

Fluency AI is an AI language learning app for speaking practice. You talk out loud in one of 40 languages, it listens to how you actually speak, answers like a person, and corrects your mistakes with the reason for each fix. It also tracks recurring pronunciation problems so you can hear the habit, not just read a score.

Why it exists

You can study a language for years and still freeze when someone at a counter asks you an ordinary question. Memorizing words and learning grammar rules are different skills from speaking in real time. Fluency AI puts the conversation first and the correction second, so you get speaking practice without losing the thread of what you were trying to say.

How it works

  1. Listen. It hears the sentence you actually said, including hesitation and accent, and underlines any word it is unsure of.
  2. Speak. You talk at your own pace. Pauses and filler words don’t break the turn.
  3. Get feedback. You see what you said, what to say instead, and why.
  4. Improve. Pronunciation feedback tells you where your tongue goes, then checks your next attempt.
  5. Become confident. The next conversation is built around the mistakes you made in the last one.

Who makes it

Fluency AI is developed and operated by an individual developer. It is a small, independent product rather than part of a large company.
