Fluency AI features

Fluency AI lets you talk the way you want to learn. Mode, correction, level and voice speed sit one tap away inside every conversation.

Which conversation modes does Fluency AI have?

Fluency Plus includes all six modes. These four are the ones to start with:

ModeWhat it does
ConversationTalk about anything. It follows your lead and keeps you speaking.
TutorIt explains, gives examples and checks you have got it before moving on.
Role-playOrder, complain, interview. It plays the other person and stays in character.
Conversation + CorrectionFree talk, with fixes arriving between turns so the story keeps going.

Casual or professional?

  • Casual: relaxed, everyday speech, the way friends talk
  • Professional: meetings, calls and interviews, in a work register

How can I control corrections?

It answers first, then shows the fix, so nothing stalls. Choose how much correction you want:

  • Off: just talk
  • Immediate: fixes as you go
  • After: fixes after its reply
  • Important only: only the mistakes worth stopping for
  • Detailed: a fuller breakdown
  • Teaching: explanations built into the conversation

Can Fluency AI explain things in my language?

Yes. Pick your language, English, or both. Ask what a word means and the explanation arrives in the language you chose.

Features: common questions

Can I control how Fluency AI corrects me?

Yes: off, immediate, after its reply, important only, detailed or teaching. You can change it mid-conversation.

Can I role-play situations?

Yes. Pick a scene or describe your own, like a job interview or complaining about a bill, and the coach stays in character.

Can explanations be in my own language?

Yes. Choose your language, English, or both, and explanations follow your choice.

Does Fluency AI give pronunciation feedback?

The coach listens for accent, speed and hesitation and marks words it is unsure of. A separate pronunciation breakdown is marked as coming soon.

Do voice and text cost the same?

Yes. Voice or text uses the same conversation time from your Fluency Plus allowance.
