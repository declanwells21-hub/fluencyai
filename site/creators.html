The pitch

We don't care about your follower count. We care whether your audience is actually learning a language.

A creator with 3,000 followers making genuinely useful German-learning videos is worth more to us than one with 100,000 followers who happen to be watching for something else entirely.

That's the whole filter. Relevance beats reach.

How it works

One loop, and it keeps turning.

Every creator we bring in becomes part of the same cycle — the more it turns, the more everyone in it earns.

1

You see Fluency

You try the app, like it, and apply to the Creator Program.

2

You make a video

Real content — a lesson, a reaction, a "how I actually learned" story. Your link is in the caption or bio.

3

A viewer downloads Fluency

They click your link, land on fluencyai.app already tagged as yours, and download the app.

4

They subscribe

Once they upgrade to a paid plan, that subscription is attributed straight back to your code — automatically.

5

You earn commission

No invoices, no chasing payment — it's tracked the moment they subscribe.

6

You make more content

And the loop turns again — for you, and for every other creator in the program.

This is more scalable than paying upfront for ads — we only pay out once a creator has actually brought in a paying user. The best-performing creator videos often go on to become our paid ads too, so great content gets a second life.
