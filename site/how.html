How Fluency AI works

Fluency AI is a speaking coach you talk to out loud. It hears how you actually speak, answers like a person, and shows the fix in three beats: what you said, what to say, and why.

What happens between your sentence and the reply?

  1. Listen. It hears the sentence you actually said, including accent, speed and hesitation, not the one a textbook expected.
  2. Speak. There is no script to read. You say what you would really say.
  3. Get feedback. It answers like a person first, then shows the fix in three beats: what you said, what to say, and why.
  4. Improve. A sound-by-sound pronunciation breakdown is coming soon.
  5. Remember. Tomorrow's conversation builds on what you got wrong today, and you control what it keeps.

How does Fluency AI know what I said?

There is no black box. You can see what it heard.

  • Uncertain words are marked. If it is only 61% sure of a word, it underlines it instead of guessing silently, so you can see exactly what was misheard.
  • Meaning comes before grammar. It works out what you were reaching for, then decides whether the slip was worth stopping for.
  • Your mistakes carry over. Tomorrow's conversation is built around what you got wrong today, and you decide what it keeps.

What can I say when I get stuck?

Every conversation carries four repair moves, so a blank mind never ends the conversation:

  • Say that again, which repeats the line once, same words, no penalty
  • Slower, please
  • Explain differently
  • Give me a phrase

What do I get after each conversation?

Ability, not points. After every conversation you see the minutes you spoke, how often you were understood first time, and the few things worth fixing, quoted from your own words. A talk streak counts a day when you speak for 2 minutes. The debrief figures on the home page are an example; your numbers come from your own sessions.

How it works: common questions

Do I need to be at a certain level?

No. Say one sentence, badly is fine, and Fluency AI works out where you are. You can also set your level yourself, from A1 to C2.

What can I say when I get stuck?

Four things: say that again, slower please, explain differently, and give me a phrase. Every conversation carries these repair moves, so you never have to give up.

Is there a streak?

A gentle one. A day counts when you speak for 2 minutes. Rest days are earned by practising, never bought.

Does Fluency AI keep my voice recordings?

Recordings are turned into text and deleted within 24 hours unless you choose to save them. You can delete any conversation at any time.

Which languages can I practise?

French, German and English are live. More are on the way; tell us which one you want when you join.
